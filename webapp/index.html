Tennis Social Game Draws

📍 Poplar Recreation Ground Tennis Court

⏰ Aug 15 Sat. 3pm-6pm

Doubles game has three rounds: Round 1, Round 2 and Final.

Rounds 1 and 2 will both be played as 7-point tie breaks.

In Round 1, it's single elimination, so if you lose, you're out.

Round 2 will be a round robin with 3 teams. Each team will play the other two teams once, and the top 2 teams will advance to the Final. Rankings will be based on total wins. If two or more teams are tied on wins, the team that has lost fewest points overall will rank higher.

The Final will be played as a full set, with the first team to 6 games winning. If the score reaches 6–6, a 7-point tie break will decide the match.
